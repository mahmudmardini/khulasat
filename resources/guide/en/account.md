# Your account and signing in {#account}

## Where your account comes from {#account-origin}

Khulasat has no self sign-up, so you will not find a "Create an account" button. Accounts are created **by invitation**:

::: owner
- **The institution owner's** account is created by the platform administrator when they add the institution. They hand you a temporary password in person: sign in with it, then change it.
- **Your team members** are invited by you from the [Team](#team) screen.
:::

::: editor
- Your institution owner sends you **a message with an invitation link**. Open it, write your name and password, and you go straight into the panel.
- The link works for **{{invitation_days}} days** only, and only once. If it has expired, ask the institution owner for a new invitation.
:::

![The invitation page: the email is already filled in; you write your name and the password twice.](shot:accept-invitation)

**Password rules**: at least eight characters, and not a common password that has already leaked online (such as `12345678`).

## Signing in {#account-login}

1. Open the sign-in page: the Khulasat address followed by `/panel/login`.
2. Type your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. If the device is your own, tick "Keep me signed in" so you are not asked for the password every time.
4. Click **Sign in**.

![The sign-in page.](shot:login)

> [!NOTE]
> If you get the password wrong many times in a row, the system pauses sign-in attempts for a few minutes to protect your account, and tells you how many seconds are left.

## Forgotten your password? {#account-reset}

1. On the sign-in page click **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your email and click **Send the link**.
3. Open the email you receive, click **Change password**, then type a new password twice.

![The password reset page.](shot:forgot-password)

If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder. The link expires after a short while and works only once.

> [!LIMIT]
> You cannot change your password from inside the panel. To change it, sign out and use "Forgotten your password?". The system cannot show you your old password, and nobody on the platform team can either.

## Panel language {#account-language}

The panel is available in four languages: Arabic, English, Turkish and Russian. Click the **globe** button at the top of the screen and choose your language. The system saves your choice in your account and opens the panel in it every time.

![The panel language menu.](shot:locale-menu)

> [!NOTE]
> The panel language is not the same as the **publishing languages**. The panel language is the language of the buttons and menus you see. The publishing languages are the languages in which summary pages are published for readers, chosen for each summary when you create it.

## Signing out {#account-logout}

Click **your institution's name** at the top of the screen. A menu opens with your name and role, and the **Sign out** button below them.

![The account menu: your name, your role and the Sign out button.](shot:user-menu)
