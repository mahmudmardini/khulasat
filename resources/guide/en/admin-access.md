# Signing in and the administrator panel {#admin-access}

## Signing in {#admin-access-login}

The administrator signs in on **the same sign-in page** institutions use (`/panel/login`), with their email and password, and the system takes them straight to the **administrator panel** (`/admin`).

> [!NOTE]
> The administrator account is separate from institution accounts and belongs to no institution, so there is no quota bar in your panel.

## The panel's layout {#admin-access-layout}

![The administrator panel: 1 the dark bar, 2 the menu, 3 the work area.](shot:admin-layout)

1. **The dark bar** at the top: its colour deliberately differs from the institution panel, so the two are never confused if you open them in two tabs. It shows your email and the **الخروج** (sign out) button.
2. **The menu**:
   - **التشغيل** (operations): الرئيسية (home), الجهات (institutions), المهامّ (jobs), الاعتراضات (complaints) and الطلبات الواردة (incoming requests). These are what you look at every day.
   - **المنصّة** (platform): النماذج (models), الكلفة (costs), **الطوابير** (queues, a technical page that opens in another tab and shows the server's waiting work), and **دليل المشرف** (this guide).
3. **The work area**.

## The halted queue bar {#admin-access-halt}

If summary creation is halted across the platform (by the spending cap or by hand), **a bar appears at the top of every screen** in your panel: «الطابور موقوفٌ الآن» (the queue is halted) with the reason, and a **إدارة سقف الإنفاق** (manage the spending cap) button. See [The spending cap](#admin-costs-cap).
