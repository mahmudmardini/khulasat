# Home: the platform right now {#admin-dashboard}

**الرئيسية** (home) is the first thing you see. It tells you at a glance whether anything on the platform needs you.

![Home: 1 the status cards, 2 recent activity.](shot:admin-dashboard)

## The status cards {#admin-dashboard-cards}

| Card | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **جهة** / **موقوفة** (institutions / paused) | The number of institutions, and how many have a paused subscription. | Open [Institutions](#admin-tenants). |
| **مهمّة** (jobs) | The number of summaries on the whole platform. | Open [Jobs](#admin-jobs). |
| **ينتظر مراجعة الجهة** (awaiting the institution's review) | Summaries waiting for their institutions to decide on verses or hadiths. | Nothing for you. The decision is the institution's. |
| **جارية الآن** (running now) | Summaries being prepared at this moment. | — |
| **متوقّفة** (stopped) | Summaries whose preparation failed. | Open them in Jobs and restart them if the fault was on our side. |
| **اعتراض مفتوح** / **مضت مهلتها** (open complaints / overdue) | Readers' complaints not yet settled, and how many are past their deadline. | Open [Complaints](#admin-takedowns) first. |
| **قراءة في ٣٠ يوماً** (reads in 30 days) | How many times all published pages were opened, with the total since the start below it. | — |

Cards in the warning colour need your attention.

## Recent activity {#admin-dashboard-activity}

A list of the latest administrative actions on the platform: who did them, when, what changed (from → to), and the written reason. It comes from the [audit log](#admin-audit).
