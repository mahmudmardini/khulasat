# System limits: what the administrator panel does not do {#admin-limits}

- **No payment gateway.** Institutions transfer money to the bank account, and you apply the plan or limits by hand.
- **No self sign-up for institutions.** You create every institution yourself and hand its owner the temporary password in person.
- **An institution's Latin identifier is not changed after it publishes**, because it is in the link of every published page.
- **No setting overrides evidence review.** No summary with unsettled evidence is published, not even by the administrator.
- **No editing page text from the panel.** The institution corrects it by regenerating, or the page is removed.
- **Provider keys and the spending cap values** are in the server's configuration, not in the panel.
- **The system sends no messages** to complainants, to people who sent requests, or to new institution owners. Contact is in your hands.
- **The administrator panel is in Arabic only.**
- **Verification sources**: the Mushaf in the narration of Hafs and the seven books. Nothing else is matched.
- **The audit log** is not edited and not exported from the panel.
- **The statistics do not identify people**: reads are page opens without cookies, and quizzes are totals without names.
