# Subscription and usage {#billing}

Open **Settings → Subscription** to see what your institution has this month and how much of it has been used.

::: editor
> [!NOTE]
> As an editor you **can see this page but cannot change anything on it**. Asking for an upgrade or higher limits is up to the institution owner.
:::

![The subscription page: 1 your plan, 2 this month's usage, 3 your subscription limits.](shot:billing)

## Your plan {#billing-plan}

The name of your institution's plan, and whether the **Carousel and image pack** are included in it. If a limit has been raised for your institution above the plan, you see "Your limits are set specially", and the numbers shown are the ones that actually apply.

## The five limits {#billing-limits}

| Limit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Summaries per month** | The monthly quota. Every new summary counts against it. |
| **Summaries per day** | The most that can start in a single day. |
| **Longest lecture** | A lecture longer than this, in minutes, is not accepted. |
| **Audio transcription minutes** | Minutes of audio the system writes down as text each month, when a lecture has no captions and no pasted text. |
| **Regenerations per summary** | How many times a single summary can be created again from its source. |

Under **This month's usage** is a bar for each limit: how much has been used out of how much. The monthly quota renews at the start of each month.

## What counts and what does not {#billing-costs}

Before any action that counts, a confirmation window tells you exactly what will be counted. In general:

| Counts | Does not count |
|---|---|
| A new summary (from the monthly quota) | Publishing, unpublishing, republishing and "Update published" |
| Regeneration (from the summary's regenerations) | Adding a language to an existing summary |
| Audio transcription minutes | Building the comprehension quiz and regenerating it |
| | Building the slides, and creating and re-creating their images |
| | Generating carousel templates |

## This month's record {#billing-ledger}

At the end of the page is a table of everything counted this month: the date, the event (new summary, regeneration or audio transcription), the summary and the amount.

![This month's record.](shot:billing-ledger)

## Upgrading and raising limits {#billing-upgrade}

There is no online payment inside Khulasat. To upgrade your plan or raise one of its limits, **get in touch with the platform management**. Once your transfer arrives, the administrator applies the new plan or limits to your institution, and you see them here straight away.

## If the subscription is paused {#billing-suspended}

If your institution's subscription is paused, you see the message "Summary production is paused". **Your published pages keep working as they are, with nothing changed**; only creating new summaries is paused, until it is reactivated.

> [!LIMIT]
> - No card payments, and no invoices to download from the panel.
> - Unused quota in a month does not carry over to the next month.
