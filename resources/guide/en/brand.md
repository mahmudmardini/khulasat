# Institution identity {#brand}

In **Institution identity** you set how your institution's pages look: the name, logo, template, colours, languages and links. Open it from **Settings → Institution identity**.

![Institution identity: 1 the identity sections, 2 the fields, 3 the live preview.](shot:brand-identity)

> [!NOTE]
> What you set here is **the default for new summaries**. Published summaries do not change until you open their preview and click **Update published**.

## The live preview {#brand-preview}

At the side of the screen is a sample page that **updates with every change, before you save**. Switch between **Phone** and **Desktop** to see both. If you change something without saving, "Unsaved changes" appears with an **Undo** button. If you try to leave the page, the system asks first so your changes are not lost.

## Name and logo {#brand-name}

- **Name in Arabic** (required): appears at the top of every summary.
- **Full name**: appears in the gathering card at the foot of the summary.
- **Name in Latin script**: used wherever Latin letters are written.
- **Logo**: a png or svg image up to **{{logo_max_kb}} KB**. Drag it into the box or choose it from your device.
  - **Make the logo background transparent**: choose this if your logo is light-coloured, so it shows clearly on the dark header.
  - **Remove the logo**: removed when you click "Save".

## Appearance: template and colours {#brand-appearance}

![The six templates and the colour palettes.](shot:brand-appearance)

The **page template** is its overall look. There are six:

| Template | Suits |
|---|---|
| **Classical** | Decorated paper, gold and Amiri script, for traditional gatherings and Islamic lessons. |
| **Modern** | A clean white background with light cards, for teaching and general content. |
| **Journal** | A newspaper feel with numbered sections, for long lessons and readers who print. |
| **Teaching lesson** | Numbered sections, a framed verse at the top and highlighted tasks, for courses and structured lessons. |
| **Brief card** | No decoration and a short format, read in a minute and shared on phones. |
| **Research** | References in the body of the page, with a side margin for citations, for those who document and review. |

**Colour palette**: six palettes. A palette works in every template, so changing the template does not cancel your colours.

## Default publishing languages {#brand-locales}

Choose the languages ticked automatically for every new summary. Arabic always, plus any of English, Turkish and Russian. They can be changed for each summary on the creation screen.

## Links and disclaimer {#brand-links}

- **YouTube channel link** and **Social page link**: shown on your institution's pages.
- **Disclaimer text**: a line shown at the foot of every summary. Leave it empty to use the default text, which says the summary is taken from the lecture and is not a word-for-word transcript of it.

Then click **Save**.

## Carousel templates {#brand-carousel}

At the end of the page is the **Carousel templates** section: designs for the slide images in your institution's identity.

![Carousel templates: a suggested template awaiting approval, and approved templates, one of them the default.](shot:brand-carousel)

1. Click **Generate templates for my identity**. The system reads your institution's name, colours and logo and suggests **three templates** in about a minute; the page updates by itself.
2. Each suggested template has a preview of sample slides and a line explaining its idea. Click **Approve** for the ones you like, or **Dismiss**.
3. **The first template you approve becomes the default** for every carousel after it. Change it with **Make default**.
4. **Remove** deletes an approved template.

- No more than **{{carousel_max_designs}} templates** can be approved. Remove one before approving another.
- **Generating templates** is a call to AI **and does not count against your summary quota**. Approving them and creating images with them is free.

> [!LIMIT]
> - You cannot upload a design file of your own or move a template's elements by hand. You choose from the suggested templates or the original one.
> - Your institution's **Latin identifier** (shown in the link of every page) cannot be changed here. The platform administrator sets it when creating the institution, and it is not changed after publishing.
