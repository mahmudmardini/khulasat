# Slides and Instagram images {#carousel}

Each summary can be turned into a **series of slides** (a carousel) to post on Instagram and similar platforms: six to ten slides, starting with the cover, then the key verse, the central idea, the main sections, a hadith, and a practical closing slide.

> [!NOTE]
> Slides and their images **are available on some plans only**. If they are not available to your institution, you see a note in their place saying they come with a higher plan. The [Subscription](#billing) page tells you whether your plan includes them.

## The slides {#carousel-slides}

Open the **Summary preview**, then the **The slides** tab.

![The slides tab: 1 the slide preview and moving between slides, 2 the image pack.](shot:preview-carousel)

- If you asked for slides when creating the summary, they are ready. If not, click **Build the slides**: they are built from the same summary **and do not count against your quota**.
- Move between the slides with the arrows under the preview.
- **Slide texts**: under the preview is the text of each slide, with a **Copy the slide text** button for each and **Copy all**.
- A slide marked **In the source wording** holds a verse or hadith copied from its source in full, never shortened.

## The image pack {#carousel-images}

The pack is a set of images ready for Instagram at **1080×1350**, numbered in order, with a `caption.txt` file holding the post text.

1. In the slides tab, go down to **Image pack**.
2. Choose the **template**: the original template, or one of your institution's approved templates in [Institution identity](#brand-carousel).
3. Click **Create images**. The slides are captured in seconds, with a bar showing progress.
4. Click **Download the pack** to download a zip file with the images and the post text.

![The image pack once created, with its download button.](shot:preview-images)

Creating images and re-creating them with any template is **free** and does not count against your quota.

## Changing the slides {#carousel-change}

| Button | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Redraw** | Draws the slides again in your current identity, with the same texts. | Free |
| **Ask for new text** | Writes the slide texts again from scratch, with different wording. | Has a cost, and asks you to confirm |
| **Recreate images** | After any change to the slides or the template. | Free |

> [!TIP]
> If the slides change after their images were created, the system warns you that the images no longer match. Re-create them; it costs nothing.

> [!LIMIT]
> - Slides are not posted to Instagram from inside Khulasat. You download the images and post them from your institution's account.
> - Slide texts cannot be edited by hand inside the system. Copy them and adjust them in your post if you like, or ask for new text.
> - If a slide's text overflows its space in a given template, the system warns you that it will be cut off in the image. Try another template or rebuild the slides.
> - Slides and their images are not built while the summary has unsettled evidence.
