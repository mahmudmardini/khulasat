# Creating a new summary {#create}

Click **New summary** in the side menu or at the top of the summaries list. A screen with four steps opens, with a **Your request** box beside it that gathers what you have chosen and reminds you of what is missing.

![The New summary screen: 1 the lecture source, 2 Your request.](shot:create-source)

The four steps:

1. [Lecture source](#create-source): where the text comes from.
2. [Gathering details](#create-details): title, speaker and date.
3. [What we produce](#create-outputs): the page, the slides, the quiz and the publishing languages.
4. [Appearance](#create-appearance): the page template and its colours.

Then you click [Start preparing](#create-start).

## Step 1: the lecture source {#create-source}

Choose one of three ways in:

### YouTube link {#create-youtube}

1. Paste the lecture's link, such as `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…`.
2. Click **Check the link**. The system reads the video's details **before spending anything** and shows you:
   - **The title**: written into "Lecture title" if that field is empty, marked "Suggested". Change it if you like.
   - **The length** in minutes.
   - **The Arabic captions that come with the video**: if they exist, the system reads the text from them directly, which is faster. If not, it listens to the audio and writes it down, which is slower.

> [!WARNING]
> If you enter a link your institution has used before, the system warns you when you check the link, names the earlier summary and links to it. If you want a second summary from it (in another language or template), tick "I know it is a duplicate" before you start.

### Audio or video file {#create-upload}

1. Drag the file into the box, or click **Choose from your device**.
2. The file uploads in parts, with a bar showing how much has gone up. If the internet drops, don't worry: what was uploaded is kept. Click **Resume upload** to carry on from where it stopped.
3. After uploading, the system checks the file and shows its length. The file name (without its extension) becomes the suggested lecture title if that field is empty.

- **Maximum size**: {{upload_max_mb}} MB.
- **Accepted types**: mp3, m4a, wav, aac, ogg and opus, and the video formats mp4, mov and webm.

### Text transcript {#create-text}

If you have the lecture's text, paste it into the box, or upload a subtitle file (srt or vtt) or a text file (txt).

![The "Text transcript" option with text pasted in.](shot:create-text)

> [!LIMIT]
> A text shorter than **{{transcript_min_words}} words** is not summarised. A short text is usually an incomplete transcript or a passage rather than the whole lecture, so preparation stops and tells you so, and nothing is taken from your quota.

## Step 2: gathering details {#create-details}

![Gathering details and the attribution style.](shot:create-details)

- **Lecture title** and **Speaker name** are required. The speaker's name appears on the page exactly as you type it: the system never adds a title such as "Sheikh" or "Dr." on its own.
- **Attribution style**: how the lecture is credited at the top of the page:
  - **In the institution's name**: the speaker's name with your institution's name (the most common).
  - **In the speaker's name alone**: without the institution's name.
  - **No attribution**: for a lecture taken from an outside source, so your institution's name does not appear at the top of the page.

  Below the options, a line shows you **how this will look on the page**.
- **Optional gathering details**: click it to show the subtitle, the Gregorian date, the Hijri date (typed exactly as you want it shown, such as "12 Rajab 1447"), the day (filled in automatically from the Gregorian date) and the time of delivery (such as "after Maghrib prayer").

## Step 3: what we produce {#create-outputs}

![Outputs and publishing languages.](shot:create-outputs)

- **The summary**: always produced. It is a published page with a link to share.
- **Instagram carousel**: six to ten slides from the same summary. It is available on some plans only; if it is locked you see that beside it. See [Slides and Instagram images](#carousel).
- **Comprehension quiz**: 5 to {{quiz_max_questions}} questions with a shareable link. See [The comprehension quiz](#quiz).
- **Publishing languages**: Arabic always, plus any of English, Turkish and Russian. Each language gets its own page. A line under the buttons tells you which approved translation of the Qur'an's meanings is used for each language.

> [!NOTE]
> Verses and hadiths are always matched against the original Arabic. Translation comes afterwards, so no verse or hadith reaches another language before it has been matched in Arabic. And verses on the translated pages are **not translated by AI**: they come from an approved, published translation.

## Step 4: appearance {#create-appearance}

The summary takes its **page template** and **colour palette** from your [Institution identity](#brand) automatically. If you want a different look for this summary only:

1. Click **Change**.
2. Choose one of the six templates and a colour palette.
3. Click **Preview the appearance** to see a sample page in the look you chose.
4. To go back, click **Back to the institution default**.

![Choosing the page template and colour palette for this summary.](shot:create-appearance)

This change does not touch your institution's settings; it applies to this summary only.

## Start preparing {#create-start}

In the **Your request** box you see the source, lecture, outputs, languages and appearance. If something required is missing, it appears under "Before you start", such as "Lecture source" or "Speaker name".

1. Click **Start preparing**.
2. A window tells you what will be counted, for example that one summary counts against your monthly quota and how many will be left.
3. Click **Start preparing** in that window to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

![The confirmation window before starting, showing the cost.](shot:create-confirm)

You then move to the [Following preparation](#progress) screen.

> [!LIMIT]
> - **Lecture length**: each plan has a maximum lecture length in minutes. A longer lecture is refused when the link or file is checked, and you are asked for a shorter one or a higher limit.
> - **Number**: each institution has a monthly quota and a daily cap of summaries. Once you reach them, no new summary starts until the limit renews. See [Subscription and usage](#billing).
> - **Lecture language**: the system is designed for lectures delivered in Arabic.
> - **YouTube links**: a single video link, not a playlist. Private or deleted videos cannot be read.
