# What the words mean {#glossary}

Words that come up again and again in Khulasat, each explained simply.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Institution** | The mosque, centre or organisation that uses Khulasat to summarise its lectures. Each institution has its own panel, team and identity. |
| **Institution owner** | The person responsible for the institution's account, who can do everything inside it. |
| **Editor** | A member of the institution's team who creates, reviews and publishes summaries. |
| **Viewer** | A member who can only see the summaries. |
| **Platform administrator** | Whoever runs all of Khulasat: the institutions, their limits, the models and the complaints. |
| **Summary** | The written page the system produces from a lecture. |
| **Source** | What you give the system: a YouTube link, an audio or video file, or the lecture text. |
| **Transcription** | Turning the spoken lecture into written text. |
| **Section** | One of the lecture's main ideas, with its own heading on the page. |
| **Evidence (citation)** | A verse, hadith or saying of a Companion that the speaker quoted in the lecture. |
| **Reference (takhrij)** | Stating where a hadith is found in the hadith books, such as "narrated by al-Tirmidhi, no. 2646". |
| **Grading** | A hadith's ruling as stated by the source: sound (sahih), good (hasan), weak (da'if) or fabricated (mawdu'). |
| **Matching** | Comparing the wording of the evidence with its wording in the source, letter by letter. |
| **Review** | Your decision on evidence that did not match its source exactly, before publishing. |
| **Publishing** | Making the summary page available online at its link. |
| **Publishing languages** | The languages in which the summary page is published for readers. |
| **Panel language** | The language of the buttons and menus you see. |
| **Template** | The overall look of the summary page. |
| **Colour palette** | The set of colours your institution's pages are drawn in. |
| **Carousel / slides** | A series of images in a row, for posting on Instagram. |
| **Quota** | The number of summaries your institution is allowed per month. |
| **Regeneration** | Creating the summary again from its source, with new wording. |
| **Plan** | The institution's subscription level, which sets its limits and what is available to it. |
::: admin
| **Impersonation (signing in as the institution)** | When the administrator sees an institution's panel exactly as its owner does, for technical support. |
| **Spending cap** | The most the platform spends on AI models per day and per month. |
| **Model** | The AI system that carries out one stage of preparation. |
| **Job** | One summary with all its preparation stages, as the administrator sees it. |
| **Queue** | The list of work waiting its turn on the server. |
:::
