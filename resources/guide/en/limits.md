# System limits: what Khulasat does not do {#limits}

Here we have gathered what the system cannot do, so you do not go looking for it or expect it. Most of it is deliberate, to protect the accuracy of the content or people's privacy.

## Content {#limits-content}

- **It never publishes a verse or hadith that did not match its source without your decision.** No setting overrides this.
- **It never grades hadiths on its own.** The grading is copied as stated in the source.
- **Its sources are limited** to the Mushaf in the narration of Hafs and the seven books (al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and the Muwatta of Malik). Anything outside them shows "We found no source for it", even if it is sound in another book.
- **Sayings of scholars** are not matched against anything.
- **It does not translate verses with AI**: it uses an approved, published translation of their meanings.
- **Lectures in Arabic**: the system is designed for lectures delivered in Arabic, and verification is done on them.
- **A summary is not a word-for-word transcript of the lecture** and does not replace the recording. A line at the foot of every page says so.

## Working on a summary {#limits-editing}

- **There is no editor for the summary text.** You cannot type inside it or delete a paragraph from it. The alternative is regenerating, which is limited per summary.
- **Slide texts cannot be edited** inside the system; you ask for new text instead.
- Nothing is posted to Instagram or anywhere else from inside the system. You download the images and post them yourself.
- No summary starts from a text shorter than {{transcript_min_words}} words, from a lecture longer than your plan's limit in minutes, or from a YouTube playlist.
- Uploaded files are limited to {{upload_max_mb}} MB.

## Account and team {#limits-account}

- **No self sign-up.** Accounts are by invitation.
- **No changing your password from inside the panel.** It is changed through "Forgotten your password?".
- **One email, one institution.**
- There is no button to close the institution's account or download a copy of its data. Ask the platform management through the [error report form](#published-complaint), as the privacy policy says.

## Subscription {#limits-billing}

- **No online payment inside the platform.** Upgrades go through contacting the management.
- The monthly quota does not carry over to the next month.
- Whatever was counted for a summary that was then cancelled or deleted is not refunded.

## Readers and quizzes {#limits-readers}

- **The system does not know who read a page or took a quiz.** No names, no emails, no cookies.
- **Visits are the number of times a page was opened, not the number of people.**
- No comments on published pages.

## Languages {#limits-languages}

- The panel is in four languages, but the **platform administrator's panel**, the **Verify tool** and the **comprehension quiz page** are in Arabic only.
- There are only four publishing languages: Arabic, English, Turkish and Russian.
