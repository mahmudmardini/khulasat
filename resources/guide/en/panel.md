# A tour of the panel {#panel}

After signing in, the **institution panel** opens. It has four parts that stay in place on every screen:

![Parts of the panel: 1 the top bar, 2 the side menu, 3 the work area, 4 the quota bar.](shot:panel-tour)

1. **The top bar**: the Khulasat logo (it takes you back to the summaries list from anywhere), the panel language button, and your institution's name, which opens the account menu and Sign out.
2. **The side menu**, in two groups:
   - **Work**: Summaries, New summary, Quizzes and the Verify tool.
   - **Settings**: Institution identity, Subscription and Team.
   - At the very end, the **User guide**.
3. **The work area**: the screen you are working on now.
4. **The quota bar** at the bottom of the screen: how many summaries you have created this month out of your institution's quota. The details are in [Subscription and usage](#billing).

::: editor
> [!NOTE]
> You see "Institution identity", "Subscription" and "Team" in the menu just as the owner does, but your permissions there are limited. See [Your permissions as an editor](#permissions).
:::

## On a phone {#panel-mobile}

The panel works on a phone just as on a computer. The side menu hides to give the screen more room; open it from the **three-line button** at the top of the screen.

![The panel on a phone.](shot:panel-mobile)

> [!TIP]
> On a medium-width screen (such as a tablet) the menu turns into icons without names. Widen the window or turn the device sideways to show the names next to them.

## The Verify tool and the user guide {#panel-links}

- **Verify tool** in the menu opens a public page outside the panel, where anyone can paste a text and see how its verses and hadiths check out. See [The Verify tool](#verify).
- **User guide** (this guide) is at the end of the side menu and at the foot of every page. It opens in a new tab straight on your role's guide, so you can read it while you work.
