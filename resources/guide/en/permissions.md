# Your permissions as an editor {#permissions}

An editor does all the day-to-day work on summaries and leaves the institution's settings to its owner.

## What you can do {#permissions-can}

- **Create summaries** from a YouTube link, an audio file or a text.
- **Review verses and hadiths** and decide on them, then continue preparation.
- **Publish**, **Update published**, **Unpublish** and **Delete permanently**.
- **Add a language** to an existing summary, and **Regenerate**.
- **Slides and their images**, the **comprehension quiz** and editing its questions, and **quiz reports**.
- The public **Verify tool**.

## What you cannot do {#permissions-cannot}

| Screen | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Institution identity** | It does not open for you. If you click it, an error page appears saying **403 — not authorised**. |
| **Subscription** | You see it, but cannot change anything on it. |
| **Team** | You see the members and their roles, but cannot invite anyone or change anyone's permissions. |

![The team screen as an editor sees it: the members, without management buttons.](shot:editor-team)

> [!NOTE]
> If you need to change the institution's logo, colours or default languages, invite a new member, or raise this month's quota, **ask the institution owner**.

## If you need another template or colour for one summary {#permissions-appearance}

You do not need "Institution identity" for that. On the [New summary](#create-appearance) screen you choose a different template and colour palette for that summary only, and the institution's setting does not change.
