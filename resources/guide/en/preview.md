# Preview and publishing {#preview}

When preparation finishes, **the summary is published automatically** at its public link. On the **Summary preview** screen you see the page exactly as readers see it, and you control its publication and sharing.

Open it from **Open the preview** on the preparation screen, or from **Open** in the summaries list.

![Summary preview: 1 publication status and "Update published", 2 the tabs, 3 screen size and preview language, 4 quick actions, 5 the page itself.](shot:preview-page)

## The tabs {#preview-tabs}

- **The page**: the summary page.
- **The slides**: the Instagram slides and their images. See [Slides and Instagram images](#carousel).
- **The quiz**: the comprehension quiz and its settings. See [The comprehension quiz](#quiz).

## Screen size and preview language {#preview-device}

- **Desktop / Tablet / Phone**: see the page at the width of the screen you choose, so you know how it looks on a reader's phone.
- **Preview language**: if the summary is published in more than one language, choose a language to see its page. A language whose translation is not finished yet is marked "Not translated yet".

![The English page at phone size.](shot:preview-locale)

## Quick actions {#preview-quick}

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy the link** | Copies the link to the published page in this language, to paste into a message or post. |
| **Share** | Opens your device's share menu (WhatsApp, Telegram…) if your device supports it. |
| **Export PDF** | Opens the print window with the page as published; choose "Save as PDF" there. |
| **Download HTML** | Downloads the page as a single file that opens without internet, which you can upload to your institution's website. |
| **Open in a tab** | Opens the whole page in another tab. |

> [!NOTE]
> "Copy the link" and "Share" do not work before the page is published in that language, because it has no link yet.

## Update published {#preview-republish}

A published page **does not change by itself** if you change your institution's logo or colours after publishing it. To give it the new identity, click **Update published** at the top of the screen. The page is made again, **its link stays the same**, and it does not count against your quota.

## Add a language {#preview-locales}

If you published the summary in Arabic only and later need another language, you do not have to prepare it again:

1. Under the preview language buttons, **Add a language** appears with the languages not yet added.
2. Click the language, then **Translate** in the confirmation window.
3. "Translating" appears, and it finishes within a minute or two. You can leave the screen.
4. If the summary is published, **the new language is published on its own** once it is ready.

!["Add a language" under the preview languages.](shot:add-locale)

The translation is made from the saved summary itself, so no stages are rerun, **and it does not count against your quota**.

## Download, publish, regenerate {#preview-more}

At the end of the preview screen is a section with this name:

![The "Download, publish, regenerate" section.](shot:preview-actions)

- **Download the page** and **Download the slide text**: files you keep on your device.
- **Manage publishing**: takes you to the [published summary screen](#preview-manage).
- **Regenerate**: the whole summary is created again from its source, with new wording. The current summary stays as it is until the new one is ready. Each summary has a limited number of regenerations depending on your plan, and the system tells you how many are left.

> [!LIMIT]
> **There is no editor for the summary text.** You cannot type inside the page or delete a paragraph from it. If you do not like the summary, regenerate it, or create it again from a more accurate source. Quiz questions, however, can be edited, as explained in [The comprehension quiz](#quiz).

## Managing publication {#preview-manage}

The **published summary** screen gathers everything about the page's publication:

![The published summary screen: the public link, and visits by language.](shot:publish-manage)

- **Public link**, with **Copy the link** and **Open the public link**.
- **Published on**: the publication date.
- **Visits**: how many times the page was opened in the last thirty days and since the start, split by language.
- **Outputs**: links to everything created (the page in each language, the slides).
- **Update published**, **Unpublish** and **Delete permanently**.

> [!NOTE]
> Visits are **the number of times the page was opened, not the number of readers**. The system uses no cookies and does not recognise devices, and keeps nothing about who opens it. Your own previews from the panel are not counted.

## Unpublishing and deleting {#preview-unpublish}

- **Unpublish**: the page is taken off the internet immediately, and anyone who opens its link sees a "This summary has been removed" page instead of an error. The summary and its evidence stay with you, and you can publish it again with one click whenever you like, **at no cost**.
- **Delete permanently**: the summary, its evidence and everything made from it are deleted **and cannot be recovered**; the quota spent on it is not returned. Anyone who opens its link afterwards also sees "This summary has been removed".

> [!WARNING]
> Both actions ask you to confirm. Be especially careful with permanent deletion: it cannot be undone.

## If your page is removed after a complaint {#preview-takedown}

A reader can send a complaint about a published page (from the "report an error" link at its foot). If the platform administrator decides it should be removed, you see a **"This page has been removed from publication"** message on the summary screen, with the reason and the date of removal. The body and evidence stay with you as they are.
