# Following preparation {#progress}

After you click "Start preparing", the **Following preparation** screen opens. It updates itself every moment, so you do not need to refresh the page.

![Following preparation while a summary is being prepared: 1 status and buttons, 2 the preparation stages, 3 glimpses of the lecture, 4 the tally so far.](shot:progress-running)

## The preparation stages {#progress-steps}

Every summary goes through stages in three groups. Next to each stage you see its state (done ✓, running now, or not started) and how long it took.

| Group | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Listening** | Extracting the lecture text | The text is taken from the captions, written down from the audio, or read from what you pasted. |
| | Cleaning up the text | Transcription mistakes are corrected and the wording is tidied. |
| **Understanding and verifying** | Extracting the structure | The lecture's central idea and main sections are identified. |
| | Extracting the evidence | The verses and hadiths the speaker quoted are picked up. |
| | Verifying the evidence | Each quotation is matched against its source, text to text. |
| | Your review of the evidence | Appears only if something needs your decision; otherwise it shows "Did not need your decision". |
| **Writing and producing** | Writing the body | The summary is written in a readable style. |
| | Building the quiz | If you asked for a comprehension quiz. |
| | Producing the page | The page is made in your institution's identity and publishing languages. |

A summary usually takes **a few minutes**, a little longer if the lecture is long or has no captions.

## Glimpses and the tally {#progress-tally}

- **Glimpses of the lecture**: once the structure is extracted, the lecture's central idea and its main sections appear one after another, before the summary is written. Move between them with the arrows, or pause the rotation.
- **So far**: the lecture's length, the time spent, the number of words in the lecture text, how many verses and hadiths were found, and the publishing languages.

## You don't have to wait {#progress-leave}

- **You can leave the page** or close the browser. The work runs on the server and finishes by itself.
- **Tell me when it is done**: click it, and your browser sends you a notification when the summary is finished or needs your review. If browser notifications are blocked on your device, the system alerts you with a sound and a mark in the tab's title.

## When it is finished {#progress-done}

The screen changes to "Preparation finished and the summary is published", with **Open the preview** and **Open the summary** buttons. The tally stays: how long the preparation took, **not counting the time it waited for your decision**.

![A finished summary: all stages done, and the tally.](shot:progress-done)

## When it is waiting for your review {#progress-review}

If preparation stopped at evidence that needs your decision, you see a message saying so and an **Open the review gate** button. Go to [Reviewing verses and hadiths](#review).

## Cancelling preparation {#progress-cancel}

Click **Cancel preparation** and confirm. The work stops and does not resume.

> [!WARNING]
> Whatever was counted from your quota for this summary **is not refunded** after cancelling.

## When preparation stops {#progress-failed}

Preparation may stop because of the source (a link that cannot be read, a file without sound, a text that is too short) or because of a passing fault. A red message explains the reason in plain words, with two buttons below it:

- **Try again**: prepares the summary again from its source. Before it starts, a window tells you whether it counts against your quota.
- **Change the source**: takes you back to the creation screen to choose another source.

![A summary that stopped because the text is too short to be a whole lecture.](shot:progress-failed)

> [!TIP]
> If a YouTube link keeps failing, try uploading the same audio file or pasting the lecture text.
