# The comprehension quiz {#quiz}

The **comprehension quiz** is a set of short questions from the lecture itself, taken by anyone through a link you share, **with no name and no sign-up**. You see its results as totals in [Quiz reports](#reports).

## Creating the quiz {#quiz-build}

Two ways:

- **When creating the summary**: tick "Comprehension quiz" in [What we produce](#create-outputs).
- **Later**: open the summary preview, then the **The quiz** tab, and click **Build a quiz**.

The quiz has 5 to {{quiz_max_questions}} questions drawn from the summary's sections and its verified verses and hadiths. **It does not count against your summary quota.**

> [!NOTE]
> No quiz is built for a summary with unsettled evidence, because questions are built from verified evidence only.

## The quiz tab {#quiz-manage}

![The quiz tab: 1 the quiz link, 2 the quiz status and when participants see the answer.](shot:preview-quiz)

- **Quiz link**: with **Copy link**, **Open the quiz** and **Report**, and the number of attempts so far. The link does not open for the public until the summary is published.
- **Quiz status**:
  - **Open**: accepts attempts, and a "Test your understanding" button appears on the summary page.
  - **Closed**: accepts no attempts, and its button disappears from the page.
- **When participants see the correct answer**: **At the end**, after finishing all the questions, or **After each question**.

## Questions and editing them {#quiz-questions}

![The list of questions: type, level and section, with the correct option marked.](shot:quiz-questions)

Each question has:

- **Its type**: Multiple choice, True or false, or **Evidence** (the participant picks the correct verse or hadith).
- **Its level**: Recall, Understanding or Application.
- **Its section** of the lecture, the **correct option** marked, and the **explanation** the participant sees after answering.

You can:

- **Edit**: change the question text, its options and its explanation, then **Save**. The options of "Evidence" questions cannot be edited because they are in their source wording, and the two options of "True or false" are fixed.
- **Delete**: the question is removed and the rest are renumbered. A quiz cannot have fewer than {{quiz_min_questions}} questions.
- **Regenerate the quiz**: entirely new questions replace the current ones and your edits.

> [!WARNING]
> **Once people have started the quiz**, no question can be deleted and the quiz cannot be regenerated, so the results do not get mixed up. Editing the text is still possible.

## What participants see {#quiz-public}

1. They open the link and see the lecture title, the number of questions, the approximate time and a **Start the quiz** button. They are not asked for their name or any information about themselves.
2. They answer one question at a time, moving with **Previous** and **Next**. Each answer is saved as soon as it is chosen.
3. They click **Finish the quiz**. An unanswered question counts as wrong, and the system warns them before finishing.
4. The **result** appears: the score, the percentage, the time, and a review of each question with its correct answer and the section of the lecture to revisit. They can **Take it again** or **Print the card**.

> [!NOTE]
> The quiz page itself is **always in Arabic**, and its questions come from the Arabic summary, whatever the publishing languages.

> [!LIMIT]
> - The quiz is **anonymous**: you do not know who answered, you cannot see one person's answers, and their device address is not stored.
> - The system does not stop the same person from retaking the quiz. Every finished attempt is counted in the report.
> - There are no certificates and no ranking of participants.
