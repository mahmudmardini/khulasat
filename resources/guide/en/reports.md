# Quiz reports {#reports}

Click **Quizzes** in the side menu to see the results of the comprehension quizzes on your institution's summaries. The results are **totals, with no names**.

## The quizzes page {#reports-index}

![The quizzes page: 1 the headline figures, 2 attempts over the last thirty days, 3 the list of your quizzes.](shot:quizzes-index)

1. **Headline figures**:
   - **Open quizzes**: how many quizzes accept attempts now.
   - **Finished attempts**: how many times one of your quizzes was finished.
   - **Average score**: the average share of correct answers.
   - **Completion rate**: of those who started a quiz, how many finished it.
2. **Finished attempts in the last 30 days**: one bar per day.
3. **Your quizzes**: for each quiz, the lecture, status, attempts, average score, median time and last attempt, with a **Report** button.

At the end of the page, **Hardest quizzes** lists the lowest averages among quizzes finished enough times, so you know which lesson needs explaining again.

> [!NOTE]
> Every finished attempt counts, including the same person taking it again. Participants are not asked for their name, so it is impossible to tell who retook it.

## The report for one quiz {#reports-show}

Click **Report** next to any quiz.

![A quiz report: the figures, the score distribution, and what needs review.](shot:quizzes-show)

- **Figures**: opens, started, finished, median and average score, median and average time.
- **Score distribution**: how many participants got each score.
- **What needs review**: the lecture's sections ordered from weakest by how often their questions were answered correctly, so you know which idea did not get through.
- **Questions**: for each question, the correct rate, the **Most chosen wrong answer** and the median answer time. Click a question to see how all its options were chosen.

![The questions with the correct rate for each and the most chosen wrong answer.](shot:quizzes-questions)

## Downloading the results {#reports-export}

Click **Download CSV** at the top of the report to download a file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, with **one row per question**: its text, its section, the correct rate, the correct answer, the most chosen wrong answer and its rate, the median answer time, and how many people chose each option.

**Manage the quiz** takes you back to the quiz tab in the summary preview.

> [!LIMIT]
> Neither the report nor the file contains any list of people or of their individual answers. This is deliberate, to protect participants' privacy.
