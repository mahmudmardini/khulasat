# Reviewing verses and hadiths {#review}

This is the most important screen in Khulasat. The system **never publishes a verse or hadith that did not match its source without a human's decision**.

## What is "evidence"? {#review-evidence}

**Evidence** (or a citation) is any verse, hadith or saying of the Companions that the speaker quoted in the lecture. The system picks it up in the exact words the speaker said, then looks for it in its sources:

- **Verses**: in the text of the Mushaf, in the narration of Hafs.
- **Hadiths**: in the seven books: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami' al-Tirmidhi, Sunan al-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah and the Muwatta of Malik.
- **Musnad Ahmad and Sunan al-Darimi** are checked too, but no hadith is published from them, because the system's copy of them has no gradings.

Then it gives each piece of evidence one of three verdicts:

| Verdict | Meaning | What happens to it |
|---|---|---|
| **Matches the source** | Found word for word in the source. | Published in the source wording, with its location or reference and grading. |
| **Close to the source wording** | Something similar was found, but with a different, missing or extra word. | Usually waits for your decision. |
| **We found no source for it** | Not in the sources available to us. | **Not published**, and removed from the summary. |

> [!NOTE]
> "We found no source for it" **is not a ruling that a hadith is fabricated**. It means the system cannot find it in the books it holds, so it cannot give a reference for it, so it does not publish it.

A hadith's grading (sound, good, weak, fabricated) **is copied exactly as stated in the source**. The system never grades a hadith on its own.

## When does a summary stop for review? {#review-when}

That is decided by the **evidence mode** the platform administrator sets for your institution:

- **Published with its grading stated** (the default): every verse or hadith whose source is known is published with its reference and grading, including weak ones with their weakness stated. Whatever needs a decision waits for you.
- **Stops for review**: only what matched its source exactly and is sound is published automatically. Everything else waits for your decision, so publishing takes a little longer.

## The review screen {#review-screen}

Open it from **Review now** in the summaries list, or **Open the review gate** on the preparation screen.

![The review screen: 1 the evidence bar and where you are in it, 2 the context from the lecture, 3 the lecture wording next to the source wording, 4 reference and grading, 5 the decision buttons.](shot:review-partial)

1. **The evidence bar**: each piece of evidence is a small block, with "Citation 4 of 8" above it. Green has been settled or passed automatically; gold is waiting for your decision.
2. **Context**: the passage of the lecture where the evidence came up, so you know what the speaker said before and after it.
3. **The comparison**: on one side the **wording as said in the lecture**, on the other the **source wording**. What is highlighted on each side is where it differs from the other. In the picture the speaker said «بالنية» and the source says «بالنيات».
4. **Reference and grading**: where the hadith is found and its grading, or the surah name and verse number.
5. **The decision buttons**.

## The three decisions {#review-decisions}

- **Use the source wording** (the blue button): the evidence is published **in its correct wording from the source**, not the speaker's. This is the right decision in most cases.
- **Keep it as said**: published in the speaker's own words. The system asks you to confirm, because you take responsibility for that wording in your institution's name.
- **Remove the citation**: it does not appear in the summary, but it stays saved in your account.

![The confirmation window when you choose "Keep it as said".](shot:review-confirm)

> [!TIP]
> You can work with the keyboard: `1`, `2` and `3` for the three decisions, and the arrow keys to move between pieces of evidence.

## Once everything is settled {#review-resume}

When nothing is left waiting, the message "Every citation is settled" appears with a **Continue processing** button. Click it and the system finishes writing the summary and publishes it, and you return to the preparation screen.

You can also move between pieces of evidence with **Back** and **Next** to see what passed automatically, such as this hadith that matched its source:

![Evidence that matches: the lecture wording and the source wording agree, with the reference and grading.](shot:review-matched)

> [!LIMIT]
> - **No summary with unsettled evidence is published.** No setting overrides this, not even for the platform administrator.
> - **Sayings of scholars** have no reference source in the system, so it makes no judgement about them.
> - **The sources are limited** to the Mushaf and the seven books. A sound hadith found in another book may show "We found no source for it".
> - The system matches wording. It does not issue fatwas or explain what a hadith means.
