# The summaries list {#summaries}

**Summaries** is the first thing you see after signing in. It holds every summary your institution has prepared, with whatever is waiting for your decision at the top.

![The summaries list: 1 Start from a link, 2 the alert for what awaits your review, 3 search and filters, 4 the summary cards.](shot:summaries-index)

## Start from a link {#summaries-quick}

At the top of the list is a small box: paste a lecture's YouTube link into it and click **Continue**, and you move to the [New summary](#create) screen with the link already filled in. It is the quickest way to start a summary.

## What awaits your review {#summaries-awaiting}

If a summary has stopped at verses or hadiths that need your decision, a yellow alert appears: "You have 1 summaries waiting for your review". Click **Show them** to see those summaries alone. None of them is published until you decide on its evidence, as explained in [Reviewing verses and hadiths](#review).

## Summary status {#summaries-status}

Each summary has a coloured badge that says where it stands:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In preparation** | The system is working on it right now. Wait a few minutes. |
| **Awaiting your review** | It stopped at a verse or hadith that needs your decision. |
| **Published** | Its page is online and its link works. |
| **Not published** | It is ready, or its publication was withdrawn, so its link does not show the summary right now. |
| **Stopped** | Preparation hit a problem. Open it to see the reason and what to do. |

## Searching, filtering and sorting {#summaries-filter}

- **The search box** looks in lecture titles and speaker names.
- **The status buttons** (All, Awaiting your review, In preparation, Published, Stopped): click one to see only the summaries in that status. The small number next to "Awaiting your review" is how many are waiting for you.
- **Month** and **Speaker**: two lists to narrow the results.
- **Order**: "What is waiting on you first" (the default), Newest, Oldest, or By title.
- **Clear filters**: shows all summaries again.

![The list filtered to "Awaiting your review", in table view.](shot:index-filtered)

## Cards or table {#summaries-view}

Choose how to view the list with the **Cards** and **Table** buttons. The table suits a long list better: title, speaker, date, status and outputs on one line. The system remembers your choice on this device.

![Table view.](shot:index-table)

On each card or row:

- **Output icons**: the page, the slides and the image pack. Coloured ones were created; faded ones were not.
- **Open** opens the summary. **Review now** appears instead if it is waiting for your review.

> [!LIMIT]
> The list has no bulk delete and no moving summaries between institutions. Each summary is managed from its own screen, as in [Managing publication](#preview-manage).
