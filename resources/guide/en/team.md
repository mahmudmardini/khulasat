# Team {#team}

In **Settings → Team** you manage who can enter your institution's panel, and with what permissions.

![The team screen: 1 Invite a member, 2 members and their permissions, 3 invitations not yet accepted.](shot:team)

## The three roles {#team-roles}

| What each role can do | Owner | Editor | Viewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| See summaries | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create summaries | Yes | Yes | No |
| Review verses and hadiths | Yes | Yes | No |
| Publish and unpublish summaries | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage the institution identity | Yes | No | No |
| Manage the subscription | Yes | No | No |
| Manage the team | Yes | No | No |

The same table is at the end of the team screen, under **What each role can do**.

## Inviting a new member {#team-invite}

1. Click **Invite a member**.
2. Type the **Invitee's email** and choose the **Permissions**: Owner, Editor or Viewer.
3. Click **Create the invitation link**.
4. The **invitation link** appears. Click **Copy the link** and send it to the person however suits you: WhatsApp, email or anything else.

![The member invitation form.](shot:team-invite)

> [!WARNING]
> **The link is shown only once.** The system does not keep it as it is, so copy it before you close the screen. If you close it before copying, create a new invitation.

The invitee opens the link, writes their name and password, and enters your panel with the permissions you chose. The link works for **{{invitation_days}} days**, then expires.

## Invitations not yet accepted {#team-pending}

They appear in a list under the members: the email, the permissions and the link's expiry date. You can **Revoke the invitation** so its link stops working immediately, then invite the person again whenever you like.

## Changing a member's permissions or removing their access {#team-manage}

- **Permissions**: choose other permissions from the list next to the member, then confirm. The change applies immediately.
- **Remove access**: the member can no longer sign in. **The summaries they created stay as they are**, because they belong to the institution, not to them.

> [!LIMIT]
> - **An institution is never left without an owner.** You cannot remove the access of the institution's last owner or change their permissions. Make another member "Owner" first.
> - You cannot change your own permissions or remove your own access from this screen.
> - **One email, one institution**: someone who is a member of one institution cannot be invited to another with the same email.
> - The system does not send the invitation message itself. You send the link.
