# Welcome to Khulasat {#welcome}

**Khulasat** turns a lesson or lecture into a well-organised written page, published in your institution's name and read on phones and computers.

You give it the lecture's YouTube link, its audio file, or its text. It pulls out the main ideas, picks up the verses and hadiths the speaker quoted, and checks each one against its source. Then it writes a tidy page with your institution's logo and colours.

## What you get from each lecture {#welcome-outputs}

- **The summary page**: a web page with a link you can share. It holds the lecture's main sections, the verses in the exact wording of the Mushaf with the surah name and verse number, and the hadiths with their references and gradings as stated in their sources.
- **The page in other languages** if you want it: English, Turkish and Russian. Verses there come with an approved, published translation of their meanings.
- **Instagram slides** and their images, ready to post, if your plan includes them.
- **A short comprehension quiz** that readers take through a shared link. You see the results, added up, in your panel.

## How a summary is made, in five steps {#welcome-flow}

1. **You give the source**: a YouTube link, an audio or video file, or the lecture text.
2. **The system works on its own** for a few minutes: it gets the text, cleans it up, finds the main sections, picks up the verses and hadiths, and matches them against their sources.
3. **You review when needed**: if a verse or hadith did not match its source exactly, the system stops and waits for your decision.
4. **The system writes the summary** and lays it out as a page in your institution's identity.
5. **You preview the page and publish it**, then share its link.

> [!NOTE]
> Matching against the sources is **not done by artificial intelligence**. The system compares the text letter by letter with a stored copy of the Mushaf and the hadith books. AI is used only for writing and summarising.

## Who is this guide for? {#welcome-who}

::: owner
This is the **Institution owner guide**. The institution owner is the person responsible for the mosque or centre's account on Khulasat. They can do everything inside their institution: create, review and publish summaries, set the institution identity, follow the subscription and add team members.
:::

::: editor
This is the **Editor guide**. An editor is a member of the institution's team: they create summaries, review their verses and hadiths, and publish them. The institution identity, subscription and team are managed by the institution owner. See [Your permissions as an editor](#permissions) for the details.
:::

You do not need any technical background. Each chapter explains one screen: what you see on it, what you do there, and what the system cannot do.

## How to use this guide {#welcome-howto}

- **Search**: type any word in the search box at the top of the page, such as "publish", "hadith" or "password", and the sections that mention it appear. You can also press the `/` key from anywhere to start searching.
- **Contents**: the list of chapters is at the side of the page. On a phone, open it from the menu button at the top of the screen.
- **A link to any section**: move the pointer over a section heading and a small chain-link button appears next to it. Click it to copy a link that opens the guide right at that section, then send it to whoever you like.
- **Language**: switch the guide's language from the globe button at the top of the page. The guide stays on the same section.
- **Printing**: the "Print the guide" button at the start prints it or saves it as a PDF.
